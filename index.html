Market replay as an inspectable experiment

A contestant sees candle data, makes a LONG/SHORT/WAIT decision, and the simulator applies execution rules: latency, slippage, fills, exits, and safety checks. The point is not that the coin-flip player is clever. The point is that even a dumb baseline can expose whether the simulator preserves causality.

baseline: seeded coin flip · data: TSLA 1Min OHLC
1. Candle context The chart shows what the player could know at that moment, not the future.
2. Decision booth The player chooses an intent. In this demo, it is intentionally dumb: seeded randomness.
3. Execution model The sim turns intent into fills and exits with declared stress: latency, slippage, misses.
4. Evidence If a result depends on missing intrabar order, it is flagged instead of silently trusted.
